# DROP LAB demo assets

These are original fictional product studies, not photographs of released CEO1.9 merchandise. The official CEO1.9 mark is supplied by the project and overlaid separately in the viewer; it is not an invented official product print.

`hoodie-study.webp` and `tshirt-study.webp` were generated with the built-in Image Gen tool on 24 October 2026. They are transparent 1536 × 1024 two-column texture atlases. The true front is in the left half and the separately generated neutral back is in the right half. Do not mirror either side. Lossy WebP compression preserves alpha and reduces the two assets to approximately 276 KiB combined. Original generated PNGs remain in the local generation directory.
